FSR Drum Glove Looper

How to use this ▸

Press the glove sensor to play a note - press harder to bend the pitch up (a smooth glide, never down). Record a first pass to set the loop length, then overdub more layers on top. Click a note to select it (shift-click or drag a box for more), drag to move the whole selection or grab an edge to stretch it, Delete/Backspace to remove it. Ctrl/Cmd+C/X/V copy, cut, paste at the position bar - drag its little flag or click empty space to move the bar. "Split" (button or S) turns clicks into cuts. Quantize snaps every note to the nearest beat, Undo/Redo cover everything above. Save/Save As downloads your loop as a file; Open loads one back in. Note lines are colored by pitch - red near C, blending through purple up to blue near B - not by instrument.
